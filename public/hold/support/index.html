Support

Hold — maternity companion by TinyKite

If you are worried right now

Contact your midwife or maternity unit straight away — especially if your baby’s movements have slowed down, stopped or changed. Never wait to see if things pick up, and never go to sleep on it. There is no wrong time to call.

Hold is not a medical device and cannot tell you whether you or your baby are well. This page is for questions about the app itself.

Quick help

  • Do I need an account? No. Hold works fully without one, forever. Signing in is optional and only tells us your email address — your logs stay on your phone either way.
  • Does it work offline? Yes. Counting, timing, patterns and reports all work in aeroplane mode.
  • Where is my data? On your phone only. We never receive your movements, contractions, notes or due date.
  • Why can’t I see Labor Mode? It appears from 37 weeks, on the Today tab. Check your due date in Settings.
  • The Pattern tab won’t name a busy time. It waits until there is enough logged to mean it — at least 20 movements across 3 days — rather than guessing from a handful of taps.
  • Sending a report to my midwife. Settings → Create a report. Pick the last 4 hours or last 7 days, then send it with your phone’s normal share sheet, or print it.
  • New phone? Records do not transfer — they were only ever on the old device. Export a report before you switch if you want to keep the history.
  • Volume-button logging works on Android only, during a counting session. iOS has no public API that permits it.

Deleting your data

On the phone: Settings → Report a pregnancy loss erases everything Hold recorded, immediately and permanently. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

Your account, if you made one: Settings → Account → Delete, or email us and we will remove it — that erases the email address and account id we hold.
