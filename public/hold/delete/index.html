Delete your account and data

Hold by TinyKite · Last updated: 31 July 2026

The short version

Hold keeps two separate things, and they are deleted separately.

  1. Your pregnancy records — movements, contractions, notes, due date. These live only on your phone. We never had a copy. Erase them in Settings, or by uninstalling the app.
  2. Your TinyKite account — only exists if you chose to sign in, and holds only your email address. Delete it in Settings → Account → Delete.

Doing one does not do the other. If you want everything gone, do both.

Erase your records from this phone

Open Hold and go to Settings → Report a pregnancy loss. This erases everything Hold has recorded — every movement, counting sitting, contraction, note and your pregnancy dates — from the phone immediately, and stops the app asking you anything else.

It is deliberately the same action whatever your reason for wanting the data gone. There is nothing to explain to us and nobody is told.

This cannot be undone, and we cannot restore it, because your records were never uploaded to us. If you want to keep a copy first, use Settings → Create a report and save the PDF somewhere before you erase.

Uninstalling Hold also removes all of this data from the phone.

Delete your TinyKite account

You only have an account if you chose to sign in — Hold works fully without one. If you did:

Option 1 — In the app (recommended)

  1. Open Hold and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete.
  3. Confirm. Your account is disabled right away and permanently deleted after a 30-day grace period. Signing back in during those 30 days cancels the deletion.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can’t sign in, email support@tinykite.co from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my Hold account.” We verify that the request comes from the account holder before acting on it, and we aim to respond within one month. The same 30-day grace period applies from the point we action the request.

What gets deleted with your account

  • Your identity record in the shared TinyKite identity service — your email address, your account identifier, and your authentication data (password hash, passkeys, two-factor secret, and any linked Google/Apple/Microsoft sign-in).

That is the complete list, because an email address and an account id are the only personal data we ever hold for Hold. No pregnancy data is involved, because none of it ever reached us.

What may remain

  • Your records on your own phone, until you erase them or uninstall the app. Deleting your account does not touch them.
  • Limited security and audit logs may be kept for a short period, or longer where the law requires, for accountability and fraud prevention.
  • Anything you already exported and sent yourself — a PDF report or a pattern card — is wherever you sent it, and is outside our control.

Timing

On the phone: immediate and irreversible.
Your account: disabled immediately · permanently deleted after 30 days · reversible by signing back in during the grace period. Backups roll off shortly after deletion.
