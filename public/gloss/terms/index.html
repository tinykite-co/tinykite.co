2. The service

Gloss is a picture dictionary for children reading real books. You look up a word and see a short meaning, a picture where one exists, and how the word is said. Gloss can also keep a list of the words a reader has met and turn them into flashcards and quizzes. A grown-up can optionally sign in with a TinyKite account and link readers, as our Privacy Policy explains.

Gloss is in beta. Features, word coverage and pictures will change as we build it.

You can back a Gloss language (for example Hinglish or American English) on our website. Payments for backing are processed by Stripe. These rules apply:

  • Until a language reaches its goal, you can cancel and get a full refund by emailing us.

7. How the content is made

Gloss’s meanings, pictures and recordings are made with the help of AI models. Every meaning is checked against a guide for what is suitable for children, and meanings meant for adults are never shown. Pictures are checked by a second model, and the ones that still fail go to a person. Translations and meanings in languages other than English are checked by native speakers before we call them finished. If you see something wrong, tap “Something wrong?” on that meaning.
