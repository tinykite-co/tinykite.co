Privacy Policy

Gloss by TinyKite · Last updated: 29 July 2026

Summary

Gloss is local-first. Word meanings come from a dictionary packed inside the app. We do not require an account. We do not run ads. We do not sell data. Two small, anonymous signals leave the device so we can improve the dictionary: a word we could not answer, and a problem you chose to report — nothing that identifies you or your device travels with either (see section 5).

1. Who we are

Gloss is published by Tiny Kite LTD (“TinyKite”, “we”). Contact: support@tinykite.co.

2. What Gloss does

Gloss is a reading companion. You type a word, and the app shows a simple meaning with pictures or short motion from an on-device lexicon.

3. Data we process on your device

  • Words you type — looked up on the device, in the moment. There are no accounts and no sync; nothing you type is stored in a TinyKite cloud account.
  • Words you have looked up — kept on the device for the “words you’ve met” list and the quiz. They never leave the device, and uninstalling the app removes them.
  • Reader profiles — a name you choose for each reader and, if you answer the optional first-run question, the year that reader was born. The year is used only to pick a starting reading level, and the question can be skipped. Both stay on the device, never leave it, and are removed by uninstalling the app.

4. What we do not collect

  • No TinyKite account, no email required to use the app
  • No advertising identifiers, no analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting SDKs in v1
  • No microphone access — Gloss does not ask for the microphone and does not record or process audio
  • No sale of personal data
  • No tracking across other companies’ apps or websites

5. Network access, and the two things Gloss sends us

Looking up words does not require the internet — every meaning is preloaded on the device. Gloss uses the network for exactly two anonymous signals, both sent to TinyKite’s own service and to no one else:

  • A word we could not answer. If you search for a word and Gloss has no entry, the app sends the word and which dictionary it was typed into (for example en-GB), so our editors can write it. Nothing else is sent: no account, no name, no device or advertising identifier, no location, no timestamps. Only single words or short phrases are ever sent — typed sentences never leave the device — and none of this happens unless you said yes to “Help grow the word pack” — asked on first run, changeable in Settings, and off until answered.
  • A problem you report. If you tap “report a problem” on an entry, the app sends which entry and which of five fixed categories you picked — nothing else. There is no free-text field: a report cannot carry anything you type, and this includes reports that were written by an older version and sent after updating. (App versions before 0.2.4 had an optional note box; a report sent from one of those older installs may still carry its note until that device updates.)

Both signals exist only to fix the dictionary. We keep them only as an editorial work queue, we do not use them for advertising or profiling, and if the device is offline they wait on the device and are sent later. Everything else in Gloss stays on the device.

6. Children

Gloss is designed to be usable by young readers with a parent or caregiver nearby. Reader profiles (a chosen name, an optional birth year for the starting reading level) live on the device only — TinyKite never receives them, builds no profile of any child, and collects no personal information from children for marketing. The app asks for no device permissions beyond network access. The two signals in section 5 carry no identifiers. The only reader-typed text that can ever leave the device is a searched word Gloss had no entry for — sent only if “Help grow the word pack” was switched on, and only when it is shaped like a word (a typed sentence never leaves the device). Problem reports carry no typed text at all.

7. Your choices

  • Uninstall the app — removes local app data from the device.
  • Ask us to delete a signal — email support@tinykite.co quoting the word or the report and we will remove it from our queue. Rows also delete themselves automatically 180 days after we last received them.

8. Third parties

Gloss does not embed advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and sends no data to third parties. The two signals in section 5 go to TinyKite’s own service only.

9. International users

Gloss is published from the United Kingdom. Word lookups are processed on-device; the only data TinyKite receives are the two anonymous signals in section 5. They are stored with our infrastructure provider, Cloudflare, on its global network, and reviewed by TinyKite from the United Kingdom. Standard connection data (such as an IP address) is processed transiently by Cloudflare to deliver the request; TinyKite's queue stores only the fields listed in section 5, plus a short-lived abuse counter keyed to a one-way hash — never the address itself — that deletes itself within minutes.

10. Changes

If we add cloud features, accounts, or analytics, we will update this page and the date above before those features ship.
